Awareness Session

CYSE 26 BATCH AWARENESS SESSION

October 19, 2026 | Events

The Cyber Security Department successfully organized an Awareness Session for the newly inducted CYSE 26 Batch. The session aimed to introduce students to the importance of cybersecurity in the modern digital era.

Faculty members discussed the curriculum, career opportunities, and the ethical responsibilities of a cybersecurity professional. Students were highly interactive and showed great enthusiasm for their upcoming academic journey.

Orientation

CYSE 26 BATCH ORIENTATION

October 18, 2026 | Events

The Department of Cyber Security hosted a warm Orientation Ceremony to welcome the CYSE 26 Batch. The event was graced by the Chairman, Dean, and esteemed faculty members.

Students were briefed about the university's code of conduct, the BS Cyber Security program structure, and the state-of-the-art laboratory facilities available to them. The orientation helped students build a connection with their teachers and peers.

Technical Seminar

AGENTIC AI SESSION FOR CYBERSECURITY

October 17, 2026 | Seminar

An insightful technical session on Agentic AI in Cybersecurity was conducted for the students. The session explored how autonomous AI agents are revolutionizing threat detection, automated response, and vulnerability management.

Students learned about the intersection of AI and security, and how modern enterprises are using AI-driven tools to defend against sophisticated cyber attacks. The session concluded with a Q&A round.

Cybersecurity Competition

HECK READY 2026 - MINI CTF

October 21, 2026 | Competition

The Cyber Security Department at QUEST successfully organized the HECK READY 2026 - Mini Capture The Flag (CTF) competition on October 21, 2026. The event brought together talented students who are passionate about ethical hacking and cybersecurity.

This Mini CTF was designed to test the practical skills of students in various domains, including Web Exploitation, Cryptography, Reverse Engineering, and Digital Forensics. Participants faced real-world challenges in a controlled environment, which helped bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and industry practices.

The competition saw overwhelming participation and enthusiasm from students across different batches. The worthy Chairman, along with faculty members, appreciated the participants for their problem-solving skills and teamwork. Such initiatives are crucial for building the next generation of cybersecurity professionals in Pakistan.
